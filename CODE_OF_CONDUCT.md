# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Respectful and constructive communication
- Graceful acceptance of feedback
- Empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or insulting language
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and public spaces when representing the project.
